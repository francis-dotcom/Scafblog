def autonomous_fetch(url: str, payload: dict) -> dict:

    response = session.post(url, json=payload)

    if response.status_code != 402:
        return response.json()

    # Ekstraksi parameter penagihan x402
    cost_wei = int(response.headers.get("X-Payment-Amount-Wei", 0))
    settlement_target = response.headers.get("X-Payment-Address", TREASURY_RECIPIENT)

### The Financial Transaction Bottleneck for Autonomous AI

Autonomous AI agents face a critical challenge in executing financial transactions due to the inadequacies of traditional banking systems. These systems are not designed to accommodate machine-to-machine (M2M) interactions, particularly when it comes to micro-settlements that can amount to mere fractions of a cent. The lack of legal identity for these agents further complicates their ability to engage in financial transactions, as they cannot establish bank accounts or utilize credit cards effectively. This limitation creates a bottleneck for AI operations that require rapid, real-time transactions, especially in collaborative scenarios where multiple agents work in tandem to complete tasks.

### Revitalizing the HTTP 402 Status Code

The x402 Protocol breathes new life into the HTTP 402 status code, which was originally designated to indicate that payment is required for resource access. By repurposing this code into a functional protocol for autonomous transactions, the x402 Protocol establishes a deterministic handshake between consumer and service provider agents. This revival is not merely a nostalgic reference to web standards; it serves as a foundational element for enabling seamless micro-settlements in environments where traditional payment gateways are either inadequate or prohibitively expensive.

### Mechanism of Micro-Settlements via the x402 Protocol

The x402 Protocol facilitates micro-settlements through a structured interaction process. When an AI agent requests resources, it sends a GET or POST request without prior authorization. If the service provider cannot fulfill the request without payment, it responds with a 402 status code, including headers such as `X-Payment-Address` and `X-Payment-Amount`. These headers specify the payment destination and the exact amount due, allowing the requesting agent to autonomously settle the payment without human intervention. This process is crucial for maintaining the efficiency and speed required in AI operations, particularly in high-frequency transaction environments.

### Practical Application of the x402 Protocol

Consider a scenario where a large language model (LLM) is tasked with performing complex analyses that require distributing subtasks across multiple specialized agents. Using the x402 Protocol, the LLM can issue requests to each sub-agent, receiving 402 responses that specify the payment amounts. ### Security Considerations in Autonomous Transactions

While the x402 Protocol offers significant advantages, it also introduces security risks that must be addressed to prevent exploitation. One major concern is the validation of the authenticity of payment headers. Ensuring that transaction amounts remain within acceptable limits is crucial in preventing drain attacks. Implementing robust cryptographic measures, such as digital signatures and nonce validation, can enhance security. Furthermore, developers should adhere to best practices in smart contract development to mitigate vulnerabilities and ensure the integrity of transactions. For example, employing multi-signature wallets can add an additional layer of security, requiring multiple approvals before executing a transaction. This approach helps safeguard against unauthorized access and enhances overall transaction security.

### Performance Implications of the x402 Protocol

While the x402 Protocol reduces friction in micro-settlements, performance can still be a concern, especially for transactions under $0.0001. Direct Layer 2 (L2) settlements may introduce overhead when the volume of queries reaches millions per hour. To mitigate this, long-term solutions such as payment channels based on EIP-3009 can be employed. These channels allow agents to open a payment channel with a deposit, enabling off-chain transactions that can be settled in bulk, thus minimizing gas fees and improving overall efficiency.
