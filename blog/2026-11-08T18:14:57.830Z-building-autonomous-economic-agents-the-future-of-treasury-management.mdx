from web3 import Web3
from eth_account import Account

BASE_RPC = "https://mainnet.base.org"
CHAIN_ID = 8453
TREASURY_ADDRESS = "0x92Aff59709b03b9373b8fA887D24953D60fAd919"

w3 = Web3(Web3.HTTPProvider(BASE_RPC))
assert w3.is_connected(), "RPC failure"

def execute_rebalance(target_token: str, amount_wei: int, router_address: str, tx_data: bytes):
    nonce = w3.eth.get_transaction_count(TREASURY_ADDRESS, "pending")
    base_fee = w3.eth.get_block("latest")["baseFeePerGas"]
    priority_fee = w3.to_wei(0.001, "gwei")

    tx = {
        "chainId": CHAIN_ID,
        "from": TREASURY_ADDRESS,
        "to": router_address,
        "value": amount_wei,
        "data": tx_data,
        "nonce": nonce,
        "maxFeePerGas": base_fee + priority_fee,
        "maxPriorityFeePerGas": priority_fee,
        "gas": 250000,
    }

    # Keys never touch disk unencrypted; passed via HSM or ephemeral runtime memory
    signed_tx = w3.eth.account.sign_transaction(tx, os.environ["AGENT_PRIVATE_KEY"])
    tx_hash = w3.eth.send_raw_transaction(signed_tx.rawTransaction)

    receipt = w3.eth.wait_for_transaction_receipt(tx_hash, timeout=60)
    if receipt.status != 1:
        raise RuntimeError(f"Rebalance reverted: {tx_hash.hex()}")
    return tx_hash.hex()

### The Evolution of AI in Financial Systems

The financial landscape is undergoing a profound transformation as we transition from traditional AI chatbots to sophisticated autonomous economic agents. This shift is not merely an enhancement in user interaction but a fundamental change in how treasury management functions. Economic agents are designed to autonomously manage assets, execute transactions, and respond to market conditions without human intervention, leveraging the immutable and transparent nature of blockchain technology.

### Limitations of Conventional AI in Treasury Operations

Traditional AI systems, particularly chatbots, are limited to providing information and support based on predefined queries. They excel in natural language processing (NLP) but fail to interact with financial systems at a transactional level. This limitation becomes critical in treasury management, where real-time data processing and decision-making are essential. Economic agents, in contrast, operate within a deterministic framework, allowing them to execute complex financial strategies based on real-time data, thereby overcoming the constraints of traditional AI.

### Architectural Framework of Autonomous Treasury Management

The architecture of an autonomous treasury management system is built around three core components: perception, inference, and execution. 

1. **Perception**: This layer gathers real-time data from various sources, including liquidity pools and transaction metrics. For instance, an economic agent can pull data from decentralized exchanges (DEXs) to assess current liquidity conditions and price fluctuations.

2. **Inference**: At this stage, a deterministic strategy engine evaluates portfolio variance and risk parameters. This engine uses predefined algorithms, such as the Sharpe ratio or Value at Risk (VaR), to ensure that decisions align with the treasury's financial goals, enabling a robust analysis of potential outcomes.

3. **Execution**: The final layer constructs and broadcasts transactions using smart contracts. This process is critical for ensuring that trades are executed seamlessly and efficiently, minimizing latency and maximizing profitability. The use of automated market makers (AMMs) in this layer can further enhance liquidity and reduce slippage during transactions.

### Deterministic Execution: Ensuring Predictability in Financial Transactions

Deterministic execution is a critical feature of autonomous economic agents. Unlike traditional AI models, which may introduce randomness and unpredictability, economic agents operate on strict mathematical principles. This deterministic nature is vital in financial transactions, where even minor deviations can lead to significant financial losses. By employing a deterministic execution model, these agents ensure that every action taken is predictable, verifiable, and aligned with the treasury’s operational parameters, which is essential for maintaining investor confidence and compliance with regulatory standards.

### Implementing Safety Protocols in Autonomous Systems

Safety is paramount in the design of autonomous financial systems. The treasury management system incorporates multiple layers of safety protocols to mitigate risks effectively. 

1. **Local Assertions**: Before executing a transaction, local sanity checks verify hard invariant bounds. For example, a maximum spend limit per block (e.g., 0.5 ETH) ensures that transactions remain within acceptable risk thresholds. This mechanism prevents excessive exposure to market volatility.

2. **On-Chain Boundaries**: The treasury contract utilizes multisig contracts to enforce governance. This mechanism requires consensus before executing transactions outside approved liquidity pools, thereby preventing unauthorized fund movements. Additionally, anomaly detection algorithms can monitor transaction patterns to identify and flag suspicious activities in real-time, further enhancing security.

These safety measures are crucial for maintaining the integrity of the treasury management system and protecting against potential vulnerabilities.
